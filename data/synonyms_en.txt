# name: English
# Starter synonyms - English
# One group per line, terms separated by commas.
# Every term in a group finds every other term, in both directions.
# Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# This list is deliberately generic: the real value comes from the terms your
# own board uses. Load it, then adapt it.
#
# NOTE: do not add antonyms. Declaring "large, small" as a group means someone
# searching for "large screen" gets the posts about small ones. Synonyms close
# the gap between how a thing is written and how it is searched for; antonyms
# widen it.
